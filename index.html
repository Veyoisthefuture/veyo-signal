The Framework
Six stages, transposed.
What the world's most demanding maisons evaluate in a boutique — applied to your subscriber journey.
Stage I Be A Host
Welcome message timing, first contact window, onboarding sequence. A subscriber who receives no personal contact within 48 hours is a scored failure — the same standard applied to a maison boutique floor.
Stage II Build A Connection
Subscriber discovery, preference tracking, recognition architecture. The difference between a subscriber and a client is whether the system remembers them.
Stage III Inspire
Brand storytelling layer of every interaction. Does content carry values and universe — or only transaction? Generic enthusiasm is explicitly scored as failure.
Stage IV Reveal
PPV reveals, exclusive drops, tier upgrades — staged as ritual, not transaction. Scarcity as theater. The product is presented, not displayed.
Stage V See Long-term
VIP architecture, tier upgrades, retention mechanics. The difference between a subscriber and a client is whether the system sees beyond the next transaction.
Stage VI After Your Visit
Re-engagement cadence, win-back systems, follow-up SLA. Every maison has a codified follow-up window. Failure to recontact is a scored failure — regardless of how strong the visit was.
